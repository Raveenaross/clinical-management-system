Welcome to TechMed: Redefining Healthcare Excellence

Welcome to TechMed, a cutting-edge healthcare facility located in the vibrant city of New York, USA. Spread across a sprawling campus of 10 acres and boasting a modern built-up area exceeding 440,000 square feet, TechMed currently offers 350 beds with plans for expansion to 500 beds.

TechMed is renowned as a premier tertiary care center within the TechMed Health System. Our hospital specializes in advanced medical specialties, including Cardiovascular Medicine, Neurology, Orthopedic Surgery (including Joint Replacements and Spine Procedures), Oncology, Emergency Medicine, and Transplant Surgery. Additionally, we provide comprehensive services in more than 40 medical disciplines, ensuring integrated care for all our patients.

At TechMed, we are committed to delivering patient-centered healthcare that encompasses prevention, treatment, rehabilitation, and health education.

Explore how TechMed is redefining healthcare excellence with innovative treatments and personalized care, all under one roof.

Our Specialities

  • Cardiovascular Medicine
    Expert care for heart conditions like coronary artery disease and heart failure. Procedures include angioplasty and cardiac catheterization.
  • Neurology
    Specialists in nervous system disorders such as stroke and epilepsy. Personalized treatment plans tailored for each patient.
  • Orthopedic Surgery
    Advanced care for joint replacements and spine procedures. Focus on restoring mobility and reducing pain.
  • Emergency Medicine
    24/7 emergency care for a wide range of medical conditions. Rapid response by skilled physicians and trauma specialists.
  • Transplant Surgery
    Leading expertise in kidney, liver, and bone marrow transplants. Comprehensive care from evaluation through recovery.
  • Comprehensive Medical Disciplines
    Over 40 specialties, ensuring integrated and holistic healthcare. Services include dermatology, gastroenterology, and more.
